## 2026-09-05 - Decorative SVGs in Close buttons\n**Learning:** Radix/Shadcn UI wrapper components often omit aria-hidden="true" on decorative icons like <X> in their close buttons, which can cause redundant/confusing announcements for screen readers even if there's an adjacent sr-only text.\n**Action:** Always verify decorative icons in close buttons or icon-only buttons include aria-hidden="true".
